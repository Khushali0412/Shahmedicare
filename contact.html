Helpful information

Before your visit

Is my appointment confirmed once I submit the form?

Not yet. The form sends a request with your preferred date and time. The clinic then contacts you by phone, WhatsApp or email to confirm the exact appointment time. Please keep your reference number (for example, APPT-20260101-AB12) for any follow-up.

What should I bring to my consultation?

Please bring any earlier prescriptions, a list of the medicines you currently take, and previous reports such as blood tests, ECGs or echocardiography results. They help Dr. Shah understand your history.

Is ECG available at the clinic?

Yes. ECG is recorded on-site and reviewed as part of your consultation.

How are echocardiography, stress test, Holter, ABPM and sleep studies done?

These investigations are arranged through affiliated providers. Your results are then reviewed with you as part of your consultation. If you are unsure which test you need, simply book a consultation — it is usually decided then.

Does the hospital offer inpatient admission?

Yes. Patients who need closer monitoring or treatment beyond an outpatient visit can be admitted at Shah Medicare Centre & Hospital. Admission is decided by the doctor after assessment.

Can I use the form for an urgent medical problem?

No. The appointment form is for appointment requests only and is not monitored for urgent medical needs.
