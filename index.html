About the physician

Meet Dr. Nishant Girishkumar Shah

Dr. Nishant Girishkumar Shah is a physician practising at Shah Medicare Centre & Hospital in Ahmedabad. He holds MRCPUK (Medicine) and looks after adults with everyday and long-term medical concerns.

His practice covers high blood pressure and heart health, diabetes, asthma, thyroid conditions, fevers and infections such as dengue, malaria, typhoid and chikungunya, along with acute and chronic illness. Care is built around clear explanations, steady follow-up and treatment plans that fit each patient's daily life.

Consultations are available on an outpatient basis, with inpatient admission at the hospital when a condition needs closer monitoring.

“Your heart deserves care that is personal, thoughtful and evidence-informed.”

— Dr. Nishant Girishkumar Shah

Cardiac services

Investigations that inform your care

ECG is recorded on-site. Echocardiography, stress test, ABPM, Holter and sleep studies are arranged through affiliated providers, with results reviewed as part of your consultation.

  • In person

    Cardiac consultation

    A thorough conversation and examination with Dr. Shah — reviewing symptoms, history, previous reports and risk factors before deciding what, if anything, needs to be investigated.

    • Review of earlier reports and medicines
    • Clear explanation of findings and next steps
    • Follow-up planned around your results
    Request a consultation
  • On-site

    ECG

    A quick, painless recording of your heart's electrical activity, taken and reviewed during your visit.

  • Affiliated

    2D Echocardiography

    Ultrasound imaging of the heart to evaluate its structure, pumping function and valves.

  • Affiliated

    Stress test

    Looks at how your heart responds to physical effort — useful when investigating chest pain.

  • Affiliated

    Holter study

    Continuous 24/48-hour ECG monitoring to capture irregular heartbeats during everyday life.

  • Affiliated

    ABPM

    Ambulatory blood pressure monitoring over 24 hours, for a truer picture than a single reading.

  • Affiliated

    Sleep study

    Evaluates sleep quality and breathing patterns, helping to identify sleep apnoea and snoring.

  • In person

    Preventive & follow-up care

    Regular reviews of blood pressure, sugar and medicines so small changes are noticed early.

  • Not sure which test you need?

    Most investigations are decided during your consultation — you don't need to choose in advance.
